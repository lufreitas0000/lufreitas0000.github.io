When an object of constant heat capacity $C$ is brought from initial temperature $T_0$ to final temperature $T_N$ by sequential contact with $N$ intermediate thermal reservoirs at equally spaced temperatures $T_k = T_0 + k\,\Delta T$, how does the total universe entropy production $\Delta S_{\text{tot}}(N)$ vanish as $N \to \infty$? This undergraduate study provides an exact finite-$N$ analytical derivation and asymptotic expansion demonstrating the $\mathcal{O}(N^{-1})$ approach to thermodynamic reversibility.
