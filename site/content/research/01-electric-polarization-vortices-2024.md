Starting from a three-orbital Hubbard–Kanamori model, we used perturbative low-energy projection to derive a spin-dependent charge-density operator in the $j_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/2$ sector. A real-space, self-consistent Majorana mean-field calculation for the extended $JK\Gamma$ model, benchmarked against exact results in the Kitaev limit, yielded the charge redistribution and electric quadrupole tensor $Q_{\alpha\beta}$ near $\mathbb{Z}_2$ vortices. We determined how exchange anisotropies affect quadrupolar vortex interactions and how a local electrostatic potential reduces the mean-field vortex gap. Electrically assisted vortex nucleation was suggested by extrapolating solutions beyond the regime of numerical convergence, not demonstrated as a controlled dynamical process.
