We studied counterpropagating chiral Majorana modes along line defects in non-Abelian Kitaev spin liquids. Earlier microscopic interface analyses established that local spin perturbations do not generate the relevant interedge mass term in the weak-defect regime, despite its allowance by continuum power counting. Building on this mechanism, we determined the field-dependent critical defect coupling within self-consistent Majorana mean-field theory. Projection of the spin operator onto the gapless defect modes predicts a local nuclear relaxation rate $1/T_1 \propto T^3$, corresponding to scaling dimension $\Delta=2$.
