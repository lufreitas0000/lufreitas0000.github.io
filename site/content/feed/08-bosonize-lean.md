[`bosonize-lean`](https://github.com/lufreitas0000/bosonize-lean) develops machine-checked algebraic results relevant to one-dimensional lattice bosonization. Its Lean 4 formalization distinguishes finite-dimensional fermionic CAR spaces from polynomial CCR constructions and records the hypotheses attached to projections, charge sectors, and finite energy budgets.

The project also contains a mathematical monograph. Its finite and coefficientwise results should not be read as general proofs of continuum bosonization, analytic limits, or interacting-state equivalence; those require additional arguments.
