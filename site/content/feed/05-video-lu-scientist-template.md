### Companion Video Script & Mathematical Notes

When we pass from a lattice spin Hamiltonian to a continuum effective field theory, it is tempting to treat every symmetry-allowed polynomial of the continuum fields as an available perturbation. In this short video and note, we unpack why the low-energy projection $O \mapsto P_{\text{low}} O P_{\text{low}}$ is not an algebra homomorphism:

$$\begin{aligned}
(P_{\text{low}} O P_{\text{low}})(P_{\text{low}} Q P_{\text{low}}) &= P_{\text{low}} O P_{\text{low}} Q P_{\text{low}} \neq P_{\text{low}} O Q P_{\text{low}}.
\end{aligned}$$
